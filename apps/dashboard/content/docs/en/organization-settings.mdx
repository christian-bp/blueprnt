Organization settings live on the General page, under Settings (the
gear at the bottom of the icon rail). Only admins see the Organization
group; editors do not see these pages at all.

## Logo

The Logo section lets you upload an image for your organization. It is
optional, but helps colleagues recognize the organization at a glance in
the organization switcher. Click the logo to choose a file,
or use "Remove logo" to take it away again.

## Organization name

Your organization's name identifies it throughout blueprnt: in the
organization switcher, and to anyone you invite.

## Country, currency, default language, and industry

The profile form below the logo edits four more fields, each saved only
when it actually changes:

Country and industry give blueprnt's AI features, including job profile
drafts, model drafts, and the assistant, company context to draw on when
generating content, so answers and drafts fit the kind of organization you
are. Industry also picks which starter set of role families is offered
during onboarding, and which criteria the library marks as recommended
for your industry in the Model area; the starter content itself displays
in whichever language the person doing onboarding currently has their
own display set to, not this setting.

Currency sets the unit every pay figure in blueprnt is shown in, across the
People register, pay mappings, and salary records.

Default language sets the language an invitation email goes out in, since
an invited person has no account of their own yet to have a language
preference. Other account emails, such as a password reset or a two-factor
code, use the recipient's own personal display-language choice once they
have made one, and fall back to this organization default only until they
do. It never changes your own display language: each person picks that
individually from the account menu, regardless of the organization's
default.

Saving requires being an admin and, other than the logo, is done from the
"Save changes" button once you have changed at least one field.

## Full-time hours per month

Full-time hours per month is what turns an hourly rate into a monthly
figure: rate times hours, wherever blueprnt compares pay. It is set from
your country when the organization is created (Sweden 165, Norway 162.5,
Denmark 160.33, Finland 162.5, elsewhere 173.33), shown as a real value
from day one, and you can change it in settings if your agreements
differ. A person's own value, set from "Edit employee" on their own page,
overrides the organization's default for that one person. A change here
reaches every live view immediately; a pay mapping already frozen keeps
the value it was made with.
