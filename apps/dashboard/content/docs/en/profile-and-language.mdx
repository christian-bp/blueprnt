Your own profile settings live on the Profile page of
[Account settings](/account/profile), reached from the account menu. They
describe you, the signed-in person, not your organization.

## Avatar

Click the avatar to upload an image from your files. An avatar is
optional but helps colleagues recognize you at a glance. Choose "Remove
avatar" to take it away again.

## Display name

"Display name" is the name your colleagues see throughout blueprnt.
Change it and choose "Save name" to update it.

## Display language

"Display language" sets the language the dashboard is shown in for you,
personally. Choosing a new language previews it immediately, with no
separate save step, and takes effect right away.

This is personal, not your organization's setting: it overrides your
organization's default language for you alone, and changing it never
affects what any colleague sees. An organization's default language, which
governs invitation emails and stands in for anyone who has not set a
language of their own, is set instead on
[Organization settings](/docs/organization-settings).
