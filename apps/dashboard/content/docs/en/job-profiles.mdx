A job profile is the standardized description of a role: what the job is and
what it involves, never who currently does it. Every role's profile has an
identity (title, function/department, team, and track) plus its purpose and
responsibilities.

## Reading and editing a profile

A role's page shows its job profile read-only until you choose "Edit" from
its actions menu. Editing swaps the identity fields, purpose, and
responsibilities for inputs; responsibilities are entered one per line and
are shown as a list once saved. Choose "Save" to keep your changes or
"Cancel" to discard them. A field left empty shows "Not filled in yet" in
read mode.

Changing a role's track resets the seniority of everyone currently holding
it, because each track has its own seniority ladder: a seniority confirmed
under the old track no longer applies under the new one, so those
assignments go back to a suggested seniority for reconfirmation.

## Why a complete profile gates evaluation

A role needs both purpose and responsibilities filled in before it can be
rated. Until then, the role's evaluation area states this precondition
directly ("Fill in purpose and responsibilities before rating") instead of
hiding the rating action. See [Evaluating a role](/docs/evaluating-a-role)
for what happens once a profile is complete.

## Filling a profile faster

While editing, an AI assistant can draft the purpose and responsibilities
from the role's identity, for you to review before saving. See
[Drafting a job profile with AI](/docs/ai-drafting).
