The Levels page, "Levels" under "Job architecture" in the sidebar, at
[/work](/work), shows how every role's evaluation result compares across
your whole organization at once. It is read-only: you cannot rate,
adjust, or archive a role from here, only see where it landed and open
it to act.

## Three views of the same roles

A tab strip switches between three arrangements of the identical set of
evaluated roles, never changing the underlying data. "Ladder" lists every
level as a row with the roles that landed in it. "Matrix" crosses level
against track, so a role sits at the intersection of its level and its
track. "Families" crosses level against role family instead of track,
one column per family. The page always reflects the current model and
ratings, so a change to either shows here immediately.

## The four zones

The twelve levels are grouped into four zones, A at the top down to D, and
every zone spans exactly three levels. Ladder and Matrix show them as four
bands: each band names its zone, says which levels it covers, and describes
what kind of work belongs there. A band folds away with the control at its
left when you want the rest of the page in view; folding one leaves the
others alone. Inside a band, each level can also show what its own position
in the zone means: the level a role enters the zone at, the established
middle, or the top. Families view puts the zones across the top instead,
since levels are its columns there.

A role sits in the zone the evaluation placed it in. That is not always the
zone its level alone would suggest: a role can be held back from a higher
zone by the model's profile requirements, and the zone shown is the one it
actually landed in.

## Filtering and grouping

A family filter lets you show or hide specific role families; it applies
across all three views, including hiding a family's own column in
Families view. Its label reads "All families" when nothing is hidden, or
how many of the total are currently shown; "Select all" and "Clear"
reset it in one step. Next to it, on Ladder and Matrix only, a "Group by
family" switch clusters each level's or cell's roles by family instead of
listing them together; it is not offered on Families view, since that
view is already grouped by family.

## Roles not yet evaluated

A role that is not yet fully evaluated has no level, so it cannot appear
in any row, cell, or column, and neither can a role whose ratings are
complete but not yet completed: completing is what reveals a level. The page
lists both kinds of role separately, in a "Not yet evaluated" zone
below the main view, the second kind badged "Ready to complete". Opening
one from there takes you to the role, where you can continue rating it
or complete it; how far its rating has already progressed is deliberately
not shown here, to keep the same blindness that applies while rating.

## Placements to review

Under the views, "Placements to review" lists the completed placements that
raise a question, and says what the question is on each row. There are three
kinds. A placement the model's profile requirements held back from a higher
zone, where the row also names the criterion that held it back, what the
zone asked for and what the role scored. An anchor role whose computed level
differs from the level you agreed for it, with both levels shown. And a role
that was assessed under an earlier version of the method.

Only the first kind is answered here: "Confirm placement" records that a
person looked at it and stands behind it, with an optional note for anything
a colleague reading the trail later would need to know. The row then leaves
the list. The other two are answered where they come from, so they take you
to the role or to its assessment; completing a role again under the current method
is what clears a stale one. The list is empty when nothing needs reviewing,
and it says so rather than disappearing.

Every confirmation is recorded in the [audit log](/docs/audit-log) as
"Assessment calibrated", including whether a note was given.
