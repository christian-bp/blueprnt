Every pay mapping (lönekartläggning) begins from the same single dialog,
opened by "Start pay mapping" on the [pay mappings](/pay-mappings) list, its
page header as well as its empty state.

## The start dialog

The dialog, "Start a pay mapping", asks for one thing: a name, such as "Pay
mapping 2026". Its description states plainly what happens next: "This
freezes a snapshot of today's pay, roles and demographics. The reference
date is today." You never choose the reference date yourself; a help note
beside the title explains that it is set to today automatically the moment
you start the mapping.

## Preconditions

Starting requires that people have already been imported into
[People](/people), that every person is classified into a role and
seniority from [Classify](/people/classify), and that every active,
staffed role in [Roles](/roles) (one with at least one person currently
assigned to it) has a locked evaluation: fully rated is not enough on
its own, since a role's level does not exist until it is locked. A role
that nobody is currently assigned to never blocks starting, and archived
roles are never counted.

You can open "Start pay mapping" at any time. If a precondition is unmet,
the dialog shows what remains instead of the name field, as a plain list
of unmet conditions, each one a link to where the work happens: importing
people when none have been imported yet, classifying the people still waiting
(with a count), or locking the evaluations still missing (with a count
and, below it, up to a handful of the specific roles involved). See
[Importing people](/docs/importing-people), [Classifying people](/docs/classifying-people),
and [Evaluating a role](/docs/evaluating-a-role) for that work.

## The frozen snapshot

Confirming captures, as of that instant, your current model's full
method evidence (the criteria with their dimensions and weight points,
the level and zone rules, the working-conditions materiality decision,
and who approved the model and when) together with every person's role,
seniority, level, and pay. That frozen copy,
never your live data, is what every chapter's comparison, chart, and
finding in this mapping reads from then on. This is why a pay mapping's
numbers stay put even after you go on to add people, evaluate more roles,
or change someone's pay: the mapping is evidence of a moment in time, not a
live report. Confirming takes you straight into the newly started mapping.
