title: How AI is used
description: Suggestion-only, always reviewed, never in the scoring path, and where AI appears in blueprnt.

---

blueprnt uses AI to help you write and organize things faster. It never
decides anything on your behalf, and it never touches the numbers a
role's evaluation is built on.

## AI never scores or decides

A role's weighting and level are always derived from the evaluation model
and the ratings a person entered, following the same fixed rules for
every role. AI is never part of that calculation, and it cannot set or
change a rating, a weight, or a level by itself.

## Always a draft, always reviewed

Every piece of AI-generated text or suggestion lands as an editable draft
for a person to look over, not a finished answer. Some features, like the
weighting review, show the proposal on its own screen before anything
changes, so you accept or decline each part explicitly. Others, like a
job profile draft, fill an editable field you review and can rewrite
before you save it yourself. Either way, nothing an AI feature produces
reaches your organization's records without a person choosing to keep it.

## Where AI appears

AI is used in six places in blueprnt:

- [Drafting a job profile](/docs/ai-drafting), for a role's purpose and
  responsibilities.
- [The AI weighting review](/docs/ai-weighting-review), proposing balanced
  moves between criteria.
- [Method documentation](/docs/method-documentation)'s "Fill using AI",
  drafting a criterion's rationale and bias review together.
- The role-families step of the [onboarding guide](/docs/onboarding-guide),
  grouping a pasted list of titles into families and tracks.
- [Importing roles](/docs/importing-roles), the same grouping for a
  pasted list added later.
- [The assistant](/docs/using-the-assistant), a chat you can ask about the
  app or your organization's own numbers. Unlike the other five, it never
  writes anything: it only reads and answers.

## Never your employees' personal data

What an AI feature sends to the model is limited to your role and
organization's own content: things like a role's title, its criteria, its
evaluation scale, and your organization's industry and country. An
individual employee's name, salary, performance, or contact details are
never included, and the assistant actively checks an outgoing message for
a full employee name before calling the model, refusing to send it if one
is found. See [Assistant privacy](/docs/assistant-privacy) for how that
check works.

## Hosted in the EU

Every model behind these features is hosted in the EU, the same as the
rest of blueprnt. See [Data residency](/docs/data-residency).
