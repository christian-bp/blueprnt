blueprnt AI is built so that no individual employee's personal details ever
reach the AI behind it, and so every use of it is accounted for.

## Never include an employee's personal details

Before any message is sent to the AI, blueprnt checks whether it
contains a full employee name. If it does, the message is refused
immediately and the AI never sees it: "The message seems to include
an employee's personal details. Remove them and ask again in general
terms." Ask about the same thing in general terms instead, for example by
role or group rather than by name.

## Small groups are never singled out

When you ask for pay statistics split by gender, a group that is too small
to report on without effectively exposing one person's own pay is never
shown as a number. blueprnt AI says the group is too small to report
instead of stating or estimating a figure for it. This protects individuals
specifically from the assistant: the app's ordinary pages, like the People
register, still show individual pay to the people who are meant to see it.

## Where the assistant runs

Like every other AI feature in blueprnt, the assistant runs on an
EU-hosted model. Your data never leaves the EU to generate a reply.

## Old conversations are deleted automatically

An archived conversation is permanently deleted 90 days after its last
activity. Switching back into a conversation from the history makes it
active again and restarts that clock. Deleting your account removes every
conversation immediately, without waiting for the 90 days.

## Every use is logged

Every reply the assistant generates is counted as AI use by your
organization, alongside the app's other AI features, so use of the
assistant is always accounted for. A message stopped by the employee-name
check is not counted, because it was never sent to the AI.
