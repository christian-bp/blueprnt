The [Method chapter](/model/method) of the Model area is where you
document why each criterion exists and review it for bias, then export
that documentation as the method appendix. Approving the model itself is
the chapter after it, [Approval](/model/approval). This is compliance evidence, not a
guarantee that the model is free of bias.

## The four statuses

Each criterion moves through four statuses: "Not started", "In
progress", "Documented", and "Approved". A criterion becomes "Documented"
once its purpose, why-relevant text, bias risk, and bias comment are all
filled in; "In progress" means some but not all of those are filled in
yet. A criterion becomes "Approved" only through an explicit sign-off,
described below.

## What you document

Opening "Document" on a criterion's row shows two sections. Rationale
holds Purpose (what the criterion measures), Why relevant (why it
matters to the work's value and why it is gender-neutral), and Overlap
with other criteria (optional, so the same thing is not weighted twice).
Bias review holds Bias risk (Low, Medium, or High), a Bias comment
(what you considered and found), and Bias mitigation (optional, the
action taken to reduce the risk).

## Drafting with AI

"Fill using AI" generates all six fields at once from the criterion's
name, description, and evaluation scale, without reading any personal
data. Nothing is saved until you review the draft and click "Save"
yourself; because AI text is filling the form, saving also requires
checking an acknowledgement that you have read and reviewed it first.
The button is not shown once a criterion is approved.

## Approving a criterion

"Approve" becomes available once a criterion is "Documented", and
requires checking an acknowledgement that you have reviewed the
documentation and confirm it is accurate. Approving locks the criterion:
its fields can no longer be edited until you choose "Reopen", which
clears the approval and its sign-off so you can edit again.

## Approving the model

Once your criteria are documented, a separate step approves the model
itself: the [Approval chapter](/model/approval) runs a twelve-item checklist,
each item Required or Recommended, before "Approve model" works. The
nine Required items cover dimension coverage, the working-conditions
decision, criterion count and dimension caps, complete anchor scales,
every criterion documented and approved, a balanced weight budget, and
valid level and zone-profile rules; three Recommended items (an
unmotivated dominant dimension, an unmotivated people-leadership
weight, an unreviewed overlap) are shown too but never block approval.

Working conditions gets its own decision on the same card: Active or
Tested, not material, each requiring a motivation; you cannot choose
Tested, not material while a working-conditions criterion is still
selected in the model.

Approving is what lets roles be rated at all (see
[Evaluating a role](/docs/evaluating-a-role)); the card then shows who
approved it and when. Any method-affecting change afterward, such as
selecting a criterion, reweighting, or changing level or zone-profile
rules, reopens approval automatically; re-approving is a fresh click
once the checklist is green again. Approving an individual criterion's
own rationale and bias review does not by itself reopen the model's
approval; the two are separate.
