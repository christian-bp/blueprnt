Classifying connects each employee to a role and seniority in your job
architecture, so their pay can be compared against others in the same role.
Open it from Classify in the People sidebar, next to the
[people register](/docs/people-register).

## How suggestions work

blueprnt groups the people in your register by the exact job title their
payroll file recorded, then suggests one role per title group by matching that
title's text against your roles' titles, and a seniority for each person
within that role's track. This matching follows fixed rules, not AI:
nothing is ever classified automatically, and every suggestion waits for
you to confirm it. A title that does not resemble any existing role closely
enough shows no suggestion at all, so you can create a role for it instead.
Revisiting Classify is always safe: it never overwrites a classification
you already confirmed, and only ever updates groups still waiting on one.

Seniority set here belongs to the person, never the role: it never affects
that role's rating, weighting, or level. See
[Weighting and levels](/docs/score-and-levels) for that boundary.

## Reading the table

Each row is one imported job title, showing how many employees share it,
the suggested (or already assigned) role, and a state: Confirmed once every
person in the group has a confirmed assignment, Unclassified if none of
them has any assignment yet, or Pending in between. Expand a row to see its
people and, for each one, change the suggested role or seniority before
confirming; a role must be picked before its seniority can be chosen.
The same split (Confirmed, Pending, Unclassified) also stands as a small
block at the foot of the People sidebar, with each state's share of the
workforce, so you can see how far classification has come from anywhere
in the area.

## Confirming

Confirm a single group from its expanded row, or select several groups and
use the bulk action to classify them all at once with their suggested roles
and seniority; either way you can still change any of them afterward. Only
a group with something left to confirm carries a selection checkbox: one
with no suggested role, or one already fully confirmed, offers nothing the
bulk action could do, so no box is shown for it. A
large selection is confirmed in stages, with a live count of how many people
have been classified. If it is interrupted, everyone already confirmed stays
confirmed, and confirming again finishes the rest.

## Creating a role for an unmatched title

When a title has no match, "Create new role" lets you create one on the
spot: fill in its title, function or department, team, and track, and it is
assigned to everyone with that title immediately. Purpose and
responsibilities can be filled in, optionally with AI drafting help, from
the new role's own page afterward; see [Job profiles](/docs/job-profiles).
