Your email address is also how you sign in, so changing it needs proof
that you control both the old and the new address before anything
actually changes.

## Requesting the change

On the Profile tab of [Account settings](/account/profile), the "Email"
card shows your current address read-only and a field for a new one:
"Changing your email requires confirmation from both your current and
new address." Enter a different address and choose "Change email".

There is no password field on this form: confirming the link sent to
your current inbox, in the next step, is itself the proof that this
request is really you.

## The two confirmations

After you submit, blueprnt shows "Check your inbox": "A confirmation link
has been sent to your current email address. Click it to authorise the
change, then click the verification link sent to your new address to
complete it." Concretely, two links go out one after the other:

1. A link to your **current** address. Clicking it authorizes the change
   but does not apply it yet.
2. Only after that, a link to your **new** address. Clicking this one is
   what actually changes your email.

Until you click the second link, you still sign in with your old
address. If you stop after the first link, your email never changes.

## If something goes wrong

An expired or already-used link shows a message telling you so, with no
partial change applied. If the new address you asked for already belongs
to another account, blueprnt still shows the same confirmation screen
either way, so requesting a change never reveals whether an address is
already registered.
