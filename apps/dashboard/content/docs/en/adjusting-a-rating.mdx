---
title: Adjusting a rating
description: Reopening a completed assessment to change a rating, and what changes are recorded.
section: evaluation
---

A role's ratings can only change while its assessment is open. Completing
an assessment settles it, so you reopen it first, on purpose, before
anything can be rated differently.

## Reopening a completed role

Choose "Re-evaluate", from beside the result or from the role's actions
menu. It takes one press: the ratings are all kept, and completing the
assessment again from the flow's last step puts the result straight back,
so there is nothing to confirm. The weighting, level, and any anchor-role
comparison disappear from the role's page immediately, and stay hidden
until you complete the assessment once more. The line above the result
says this before you press.

## Changing a rating

Once reopened, choose "Adjust ratings" from the same menu. This opens
the same stepper described in
[Evaluating a role](/docs/evaluating-a-role), starting again at the
first criterion, but pre-filled: every criterion already shows the step
you picked and any motivation you wrote, so you are reviewing and
editing your existing judgment rather than starting from nothing. The
weighting and level stay hidden while you step through, exactly as they
do the first time. Changing a step and choosing "Next criterion" saves
that criterion immediately, one at a time; submitting the same step and
motivation that are already there changes nothing, and no audit entry
is created.

You see the result again only once you complete the assessment once more,
on the flow's last step, exactly as the first time. A fully rated role opens at the first
criterion like any other, so completing again is a walk forward over
answers already selected.

## When adjusting is blocked

Reopening always requires the role's assessment to already be completed;
there is nothing to reopen otherwise. Once a role is archived, it can no
longer be rated, reopened, or adjusted at all: "Re-evaluate" no longer
appears on its menu, and archiving is one-way, so there is no way to open
an archived role for further rating; see
[Archiving a role](/docs/archiving-roles) for what else becomes
read-only. Rating still requires your organization's model to be
approved, the same precondition as the first time; this normally stays
satisfied once a role already has a completed assessment to adjust, unless
a method change has reopened approval in the meantime.

## What gets logged

Every saved change to a rating is recorded in the audit log as "Rating
changed", with the criterion's before and after value and motivation. If
the change moves the role to a different level, blueprnt also logs a
separate "Level recalibrated" entry pointing back to the rating that
caused it, so the history of why a role's level moved stays visible
alongside the ratings themselves. Reopening and completing again are each
their own audited actions too.
