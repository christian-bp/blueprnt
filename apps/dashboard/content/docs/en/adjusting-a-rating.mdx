---
title: Adjusting a rating
description: Unlocking a locked assessment to change a rating, and what changes are recorded.
section: evaluation
---

A role's ratings can only change while its assessment is unlocked. A
locked assessment protects a revealed result from casual edits: you
unlock it first, on purpose, before anything can be rated differently.

## Unlocking a locked role

From a locked role's actions menu, choose "Unlock assessment". A confirm
dialog explains what happens: unlocking hides the weighting and level
again until the assessment is locked once more, and ratings can then be
changed. Confirming with "Yes, unlock" does exactly that; the weighting,
level, and any anchor-role comparison disappear from the role's page
immediately.

## Changing a rating

Once unlocked, choose "Adjust ratings" from the same menu. This opens
the same stepper described in
[Evaluating a role](/docs/evaluating-a-role), starting again at the
first criterion, but pre-filled: every criterion already shows the step
you picked and any motivation you wrote, so you are reviewing and
editing your existing judgment rather than starting from nothing. The
weighting and level stay hidden while you step through, exactly as they
do the first time. Changing a step and choosing "Next criterion" saves
that criterion immediately, one at a time; submitting the same step and
motivation that are already there changes nothing, and no audit entry
is created.

You see the result again only once you lock the assessment once more,
the same "Lock assessment" action as the first time, available once
every criterion has a rating.

## When adjusting is blocked

Unlocking always requires the role's assessment to already be locked;
there is nothing to unlock otherwise. Once a role is archived, it can no
longer be rated, unlocked, or adjusted at all: "Unlock assessment" no
longer appears on its menu, and archiving is one-way, so there is no way
to reopen an archived role for further rating; see
[Archiving a role](/docs/archiving-roles) for what else becomes
read-only. Rating still requires your organization's model to be
approved, the same precondition as the first time; this normally stays
satisfied once a role already has a locked evaluation to adjust, unless
a method change has reopened approval in the meantime.

## What gets logged

Every saved change to a rating is recorded in the audit log as "Rating
changed", with the criterion's before and after value and motivation. If
the change moves the role to a different level, blueprnt also logs a
separate "Level recalibrated" entry pointing back to the rating that
caused it, so the history of why a role's level moved stays visible
alongside the ratings themselves. Unlocking and locking again are each
their own audited actions too.
