---
title: Erasing a person
description: The permanent, type-to-confirm deletion of an employee, and what is anonymized rather than kept.
section: people
---

Erasing an employee is a true, permanent deletion, not an archive: it
exists for the [GDPR right to erasure](/docs/gdpr-and-erasure), so there
is no way to undo it or bring the person back afterward.

Their salary records and role assignments are deleted outright. Where
their identity still appears afterward, in the audit trail or in the
snapshot of an already finalized pay mapping, it is pseudonymized
rather than kept, while the aggregate figures it contributed to
remain; see "What is anonymized rather than deleted" below for the
detail.

## Erasing one person

Only an admin can erase a person, so the controls below appear for
admins alone. An editor who needs an erasure asks an admin to do it; see
[Members and roles](/docs/members-and-roles).

From that person's actions menu, choose "Delete employee". The dialog asks
you to type a confirmation word before the delete button turns on: the
person's own employee number if they have one, otherwise the word "DELETE".
Typing anything else simply leaves the action disabled, rather than showing
an error. Confirming deletes the person together with every salary record
and role assignment they have.

## Erasing several at once

Select rows on the [people register](/docs/people-register), then choose
the delete action naming how many employees are selected; this also asks
you to type "DELETE" to confirm. The
people are erased one at a time, with a live progress count. If something
interrupts it partway through, whoever was already erased stays erased;
confirming again finishes whoever is left.

## What is anonymized rather than deleted

The audit trail is kept for accountability rather than deleted along with
the person. Erasing someone tombstones their identity: name, gender,
employee number, birth date, and job title, everywhere it appears in their
earlier audit history, so the trail no longer names them; entries that
describe the change itself, such as dates or department, remain. If that
person was ever included in a finalized pay mapping snapshot, their
identity there is pseudonymized the same way, while the aggregate figures
it contributed to are kept intact.

## What erasure does not do

Erasing a person does not block their employee number from being reused: a
later payroll import using the same number simply creates a new person
record, since nothing records that the number was ever erased.
