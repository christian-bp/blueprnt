A pay mapping (lönekartläggning) carries one of four statuses: Active,
Paused, Under review, or Completed. It starts Active the moment you create
it; today the app only ever moves a mapping from Active straight to
Completed, and back again if you reopen it. Paused and Under review exist
for a longer lifecycle, but nothing in the app currently moves a mapping
into either one.

## Switching between runs

Once you are inside a pay mapping, its name and status show at the top of
the page and double as a switcher: opening it lists every pay mapping your
organization has, so you can jump to the same tab or chapter in a
different mapping to compare them, or step back to the full
[list](/pay-mappings).

## Completing a pay mapping

A pay mapping can only be completed once every required step across all
four analysis chapters is documented: every equal-work group that needs
it, every women-dominated group that needs it, every applicable rules and
practice area, and both fields of the collaboration record. Until then,
the action that would complete it shows how many steps remain instead.
Completing it locks the mapping; a "Reopen" action, confirmed before it
takes effect, returns it to Active for further edits, after which it must
be completed again.

## Read-only after completion

Once completed, every analysis step, the collaboration record, and the
content of every action and note become read-only. Only an action's own
status can still change afterward, since the action plan it belongs to is
meant to be followed up for as long as it takes to carry out.

## How many runs an org keeps

An organization can start as many pay mappings as it wants over time, one
for each year's cycle, and nothing in blueprnt limits or removes older
ones automatically. A mapping can be renamed at any time regardless of its
status, since its name is not part of the frozen evidence it holds.
Deleting one permanently removes it together with its frozen snapshot and
all of its documentation, actions, and notes, and this cannot be undone.
