Every member of your organization holds one of two roles: Admin or Editor.

## What an editor can do

An Editor does the organization's own work: registering roles and rating
them, building the evaluation model and approving it, importing and
managing people, and running the pay mapping. Everything except
administering the organization itself.

## What an admin can do

An Admin can do everything an Editor can, plus administer the
organization itself: its settings and name, and the member list
(inviting people, changing their role, and removing them). Admins are
also the only members who can read the audit log, and the only ones who
see the Organization page at all; the sidebar's Administration group
only appears for admins.

## Viewing and changing a member's role

The Members tab lists every active member alongside any pending
invitations, which are marked "Pending". Each member's "..." menu offers
"Make admin" or "Make editor" to switch their role, and "Remove from
organization" to take away their access entirely.

## The last-admin guard

An organization can never be left without an admin, since there would be no
way back in to fix it from inside the app. If a member is the organization's
only admin, blueprnt disables demoting or removing them, with a note
explaining why: "The only admin cannot be removed or demoted. Add another
admin first." Make someone else an admin first, and the original admin can
then be changed or removed like any other member.
