---
title: Archiving a role
description: What archiving locks on a role, and what stays visible afterward.
section: roles
---

Archiving removes a role from the active register and from results,
while keeping its data and history. Use it for a role your organization
no longer needs to evaluate, rather than deleting anything.

## Who can archive, and how

Archiving a role is member-level work, from "Archive role" in the role's
actions menu. A confirmation states plainly that archiving removes the
role from the active list and all results, and that its data and history
are kept. Archiving is one-way: there is no action to reopen an archived
role.

## What becomes read-only

An archived role turns read-only everywhere. Its job profile can no
longer be edited, the AI drafting assistant no longer applies to it, and
it can no longer be rated or adjusted. If the role was an anchor role,
its status becomes "Replaced" rather than being deleted, so the
calibration history it was part of stays auditable.

## What stays visible

An archived role's page still shows its job profile and, if it was fully
evaluated, its past result. A note on the page states that the role is
archived and read-only, and that its history is kept, so nothing about a
past evaluation disappears just because the role is no longer active.
