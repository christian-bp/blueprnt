blueprnt AI is a chat assistant you can ask about the app, its concepts, and
your own organization's numbers. It only reads, it never changes anything:
sending it a message can never create, edit, or delete data anywhere else in
blueprnt.

## Two ways in

The [Overview](/) page opens with a prompt box in its hero, below your
greeting and status line and above the action cards. Typing a question there
and pressing Enter, or the send button, always starts a brand new conversation:
if you had an earlier conversation open, it is set aside first. You are then
taken to the Assistant page, where the reply is already streaming in.

The Assistant page itself, reached from the icon rail as
"Assistant", is the full conversation view: a history panel on the left and
the current conversation on the right. Use it directly when you want to
continue a past conversation or start one without going through Overview.

## Starting a conversation

The input box's placeholder reads "How can I help you today?". Below it,
three starter questions are offered as buttons: "What is a criterion?", "How
has our pay gap developed?", and "How do I run a pay mapping?". Clicking one
sends it immediately, the same as typing your own question and pressing
Enter.

Only one conversation can be active for you at a time, and only one reply
can be generating at once. If you try to send a new message while a reply is
still streaming, blueprnt AI asks you to wait for it to finish or to stop it
first.

## Conversation history

The history panel lists your past conversations, each with an automatically
generated title, grouped by day. It is yours alone: other members of your
organization do not see your conversations, only their own.

The panel's "New conversation" button sets the current conversation aside
and starts a fresh one. A conversation's "..." menu offers "Rename
conversation", to give it a title of your choosing, and "Delete
conversation", which permanently removes every message in it after you
confirm "Yes, delete". The panel itself can be collapsed to give the
conversation more room, and expanded again from the icon that takes its
place.

## Stopping a reply

While blueprnt AI is generating a reply, the send button turns into a stop
control. Pressing it stops the reply where it stands: whatever text and
charts had already appeared stay on screen, marked as stopped, and you are
free to send a new message right away.
