Importing roles adds many roles at once from a pasted list, instead of
creating them one by one. Start from "Add roles" on the [Roles](/roles) page,
which opens a full-screen wizard.

## Paste your roles

Paste whatever you have: a plain list of titles, a team-by-team breakdown, or
a column copied from a spreadsheet. Separate roles with commas, or group them
by starting with a family name and a colon. There is no file upload; pasted
text only, up to 20,000 characters.

## What the AI does

The AI groups your pasted roles into role families and matches each one to a
track. Where a family name matches one you already have, it reuses that
family instead of creating a duplicate.

## Reviewing before you commit

The review screen shows your organization's whole register as one table,
grouped by family. Roles that already exist render read-only, with nothing
to edit or remove, so it is clear at a glance that they are already part of
your register. The new roles the AI proposed are editable: adjust a title or
change its track before confirming. A proposed role whose title already
exists in its family is left out of the review entirely and counted as
already existing.

An import takes at most 100 roles across 20 role families at a time. If your
pasted list generates more than that, the extra roles or families are left
out before the review renders, so add the rest in a second import. If you
push a reviewed list past either cap yourself, by adding rows during review,
confirming is blocked until you remove enough rows to fit. Nothing is saved
until you confirm; leaving the review prompts you to discard it first.

## After you confirm

Confirming creates the new families and roles. blueprnt then drafts a job
profile for each newly created role automatically, showing progress while it
works. The import is purely additive: it never renames, archives, or
re-tracks anything that already existed in your register.
