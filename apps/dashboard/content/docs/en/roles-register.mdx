The role register lists every job your organization evaluates. Open it from
[Roles](/roles). Each role describes a job, never the person doing it: the
same role can gain or lose its holder without changing.

## Reading the table

Roles are grouped by role family, each group showing the family's name and
role count; roles with no family sit together at the end under "No family".
Within a group, every row shows the role's title, its track, its team, how
many employees currently hold it, and its level once evaluation is complete.
A role that has not been fully evaluated, or whose evaluation is complete
but not yet locked, shows "Not yet evaluated" instead of a level. There is no separate sort order or pagination: the family grouping
is the table's order.

Use the search field to filter by title, team, or function, or narrow to one
track with the track filter. A result count such as "12 of 40 roles" appears
once a search or filter is active, alongside a way to clear it.

## Adding roles

Two actions sit above the table. "Create role" opens a dialog for a single
role: fill in its title, function, team, and track, and optionally its
purpose and responsibilities right away. "Add roles" opens the import wizard,
the faster path when you have several roles to add at once; see
[Importing roles](/docs/importing-roles).

## Opening a role

Selecting a role's title opens its role page, where you complete its job
profile and evaluate it. See [Job profiles](/docs/job-profiles) for what
lives there.
