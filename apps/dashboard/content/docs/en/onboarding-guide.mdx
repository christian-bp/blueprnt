The first time an admin signs in to a new organization, blueprnt walks them
through a short setup wizard before showing the rest of the app. The wizard
has four steps: name, country, industry, and role families. There is no
separate step for choosing a language or an evaluation model; both are
handled for you, as explained below.

## Organization name

The first screen asks "What is your organization called?". Enter a name and
choose "Next" to create the organization. Revisiting this step later only
renames the organization if you change the name.

## Country

The second screen asks "Where are you based?" with option cards for Sweden,
Norway, Denmark, Finland, and Other. Picking a country moves you straight to
the next screen and also sets your organization's default currency and
default language, both derived from the country you chose. There is no
separate language question: change the default language later from
[Organization](/organization) if you need to.

## Industry

The third screen asks "Which industry are you in?" with nine option cards:
Public sector, Manufacturing, Consulting, Retail, IT/Telecom, Healthcare &
wellness, Finance & insurance, Real estate & construction, and Other.
Picking an industry moves you on immediately. Your industry also
determines the starter set offered in the next step.

Before you reach the role families step, blueprnt creates an empty
evaluation model for your organization behind the scenes: no criteria
selected yet, nothing to review at this point. Building it, selecting
criteria from blueprnt's library, documenting and weighting them, and
approving the result, is separate work you do afterward from
[Model](/model); see [Model overview](/docs/model-overview).

## Role families

The last step asks what role families and roles exist at your
organization. Track (individual contributor, lead, or manager) is what
kind of job the role is; family is just how you group roles in your own
register, and the two are independent. This step offers two ways to fill
your role register:

- Type or paste a list of role titles, one per line or comma separated,
  straight from a document, spreadsheet, or HR system. blueprnt's AI
  groups them into role families and suggests each role's track. Paste
  titles only, never employee names.
- **Use an industry template**: choose "Use an industry template" to start
  from a ready-made set of families and roles for your chosen industry,
  without using AI at all.

Either path lands on the same review table, fully editable: rename
families, move roles between them, change a role's suggested track, or
remove anything you do not want. Nothing is saved to your organization
until you choose "Next" from this review. A set already saved (for example
if you reload mid-review) can be discarded with "Start over", which asks
you to confirm "Yes, start over" before it clears the set.

Once you proceed, blueprnt creates the families and roles you reviewed,
then automatically drafts a purpose and a set of responsibilities for each
new role from its title, shown as "Drafting role profiles" with a progress
bar while it works. You can edit any of these drafts afterward from
[Roles](/roles).

## What happens next

Once you finish, blueprnt takes you to the dashboard. With no employees
imported yet, its top to-do is "Get started with your employees";
alongside it sits "Ready to approve the model", since your model
already exists but starts with no criteria selected. There is no
separate to-do for selecting or weighting criteria: that work happens
in the [Model](/model) area itself, chapter by chapter, and the
approve-model card leads you there. Roles cannot be rated until the model is approved. See
[Model overview](/docs/model-overview) and
[Method documentation](/docs/method-documentation) for how that work
goes.

## If you reload partway through

Reloading the page always takes you back to the first step you have not
finished, including partway through the role families step. You never
lose progress by refreshing.

## If you are not the admin

Setup can only be completed by an admin. Any other member who signs in to
an organization that has not finished setup sees a waiting screen instead
of the wizard until an admin finishes it.
