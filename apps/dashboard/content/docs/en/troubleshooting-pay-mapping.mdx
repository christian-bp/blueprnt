This page explains every message that can appear while starting,
documenting, or completing a pay mapping (lönekartläggning).

## I can't start a pay mapping

Code: `payMappingPreconditionsUnmet`

"People or roles are missing classification or evaluation, so the pay
mapping cannot start yet."

Starting a pay mapping needs every active person classified into a role
and seniority, and every active, staffed role fully evaluated. The start
dialog already replaces its name field with a plain list of exactly what
is unmet, each item a link to where the work happens, so you should rarely
see this message itself: it mainly backstops a race, for example two
admins opening the dialog before either's precondition data has
refreshed. See [Starting a pay mapping](/docs/starting-a-pay-mapping),
[Classifying people](/docs/classifying-people), and
[Evaluating a role](/docs/evaluating-a-role) for the work itself.

## I can't edit notes or documentation on a completed pay mapping

Code: `payMappingRunCompleted`

"The pay mapping is completed and locked. Reopen it to edit."

Once a pay mapping is completed, its analysis steps, collaboration record,
and the content of its actions and notes become read-only. Choose
"Reopen" from the pay mapping's overview to unlock it for editing, then
complete it again when you are done. An action's own status can still be
changed even on a completed mapping; only content edits are locked. See
[Pay mapping lifecycle and statuses](/docs/run-lifecycle).

## I can't mark an equal-work group as done

Code: `payMappingDocumentationRequired`

"Add an objective reason or a deepened analysis before marking the group
done."

A group only carries this duty when it has at least one woman and one man
and the women's average pay trails the men's; other groups never need it.
Choose at least one objective reason for the pay difference, add a
free-text note, or both, before marking the group done. See
[Equal work](/docs/equal-work).

## I can't complete the pay mapping

Code: `payMappingGateUnmet`

"Steps remain in the review before the pay mapping can be completed."

A pay mapping can only be completed once every required step across all
four analysis chapters is documented. The action that would complete it
already shows how many steps remain, so you should rarely see this
message directly; finish the outstanding steps it lists. See
[Pay mapping lifecycle and statuses](/docs/run-lifecycle).
