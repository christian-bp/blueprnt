This page explains every message that can appear while building the
evaluation model, rating a role, or using AI drafting and the Blueprnt AI
assistant.

## I can't create a new evaluation model

Code: `modelExists`

"This organization already has an evaluation model."

Your organization already has exactly one evaluation model; blueprnt never
allows a second one. In practice you will not see this from anywhere in the
finished app, since the model is created for you once during onboarding
and there is no "create model" action afterward; it can only happen if two
admins somehow complete onboarding's model step at the exact same moment.
If your organization already has a model, edit it from
[Model overview](/docs/model-overview) instead.

## I cannot save my weighting

Code: `weightsUnbalanced`

"The weighting must match the point budget."

Every criterion's weight points must add up exactly to the model's point
budget (three points per criterion) before "Save weighting" will do
anything. The Weighting tab already shows this as a running count of
points left to distribute, or points over the budget, so you should rarely
hit this message itself; adjust weight points until that count reads zero.
See [Weighting and the point budget](/docs/weighting-and-point-budget).

## I can't remove a criterion, or finish onboarding

Code: `tooFewCriteria`

"A model needs at least 5 criteria."

A model must always keep at least five criteria. Once your organization
has finished onboarding, the "Remove" action on the fifth-to-last criterion
becomes unavailable for exactly this reason. Add criteria back until you
have at least five. See
[Criteria and the evaluation scale](/docs/criteria-and-scale).

## I can't edit or rate something because it says it's locked

### The role is locked

Code: `roleLocked`

"This role is archived and read-only. Archiving cannot be undone, so
create a new role if this work still needs evaluating."

An archived role is closed to every change: rating it, editing its job
profile, or managing it as an anchor role. The app's own edit and rate
controls are already disabled once a role is archived, so seeing this
message usually means the role was archived in another tab or by a
colleague while you had it open for editing; reload the role's page. Its
job profile, history and past result stay visible, and if the work itself
still needs evaluating, create a new role for it. See
[Archiving a role](/docs/archiving-roles).

### The criterion is locked

Code: `criterionLocked`

"This criterion is approved and locked. Reopen it first."

Once a criterion's method documentation is approved, its rationale and
bias-review text can no longer be edited. Choose "Reopen" on the Model
page's Method tab to clear the approval and its sign-off, edit the text, then approve it
again. See [Method documentation](/docs/method-documentation).

## I can't set a role as an anchor role

Code: `ratingsIncomplete`

"All criteria must be rated first."

Designating a role as an anchor role, or setting its agreed level, needs a
complete evaluation: every criterion rated. Finish rating the role first;
see [Evaluating a role](/docs/evaluating-a-role) and
[Anchor roles](/docs/anchor-roles).

## I can't rate a role, or generate an AI suggestion

Code: `profileIncomplete`

"Complete the company profile first."

This covers two related preconditions. Rating a role needs its job
profile's purpose and responsibilities filled in first; the role's
evaluation area already states this directly ("Fill in purpose and
responsibilities before rating") in place of the Rate action, so you
should rarely see the message itself. See
[Job profiles](/docs/job-profiles). Some AI generation calls also need
your organization's own company profile (such as industry and country)
filled in before they can run; finish that from
[Organization settings](/docs/organization-settings) if an AI draft
refuses to generate for this reason.

## I'm told that status change isn't allowed

Code: `invalidTransition`

"That status change is not allowed."

A generic message for an action that does not apply to the entity's
current status, for example applying an AI suggestion to a role that has
moved on to a different state, or acting on a pay mapping that is not
currently Active (see [Pay mapping lifecycle and statuses](/docs/run-lifecycle)).
Reload the page to see the entity's current status, and use the action
that matches it.

## I can't create a role or family because the name already exists

### The family name is taken

Code: `roleFamilyExists`

"A family with that name already exists."

Every role family's name must be unique in your organization, regardless
of case. Pick a different name; see [Role families](/docs/role-families).

### The role title is taken

Code: `roleExists`

"A role with that name already exists in this family."

A role's title must be unique within its family. Pick a different title,
or place the role in a different family; see
[The role register](/docs/roles-register).

## AI suggestions aren't working

### AI is not configured

Code: `aiUnavailable`

"AI is not switched on for your organization. Contact support to turn it
on."

AI drafting (job profiles, method documentation, weighting review) and the
Blueprnt AI assistant are not available to your organization, and you
cannot switch them on yourself. Contact support to have them turned on. In
the meantime every field an AI draft would fill can be written by hand.

### The suggestion could not be generated

Code: `aiGenerationFailed`

"The AI suggestion could not be generated."

The AI call ran but did not return a usable result. Try generating the
draft again; if it keeps failing, fall back to writing the field by hand.
See [Drafting a job profile with AI](/docs/ai-drafting).

## I'm having trouble with the Blueprnt AI assistant chat

The Blueprnt AI chat assistant (reached from [Assistant](/assistant)) is a
separate feature from the AI drafting above: it answers questions about
the app and your organization's own numbers rather than filling in fields.

### A reply is still generating

Code: `assistantBusy`

"Blueprnt AI is still answering. Wait for it to finish or stop it."

Only one reply can generate at a time. The send button already turns into
a stop control while a reply streams, so this message is mainly a
backstop for sending twice very quickly; wait for the current reply, or
press stop, then send again. See [Using the assistant](/docs/using-the-assistant).

### Too many messages in a short time

Code: `assistantRateLimited`

"You have sent many messages in a short time. Try again in a little
while."

There is a cap on how many messages one person can send within a rolling
hour. Wait for the window to clear before sending more.

### The message is empty

Code: `assistantInvalidMessage`

"Write a message before sending."

The message was empty after removing leading and trailing whitespace. Type
something before sending.

### The message contains personal details

Code: `assistantPersonalData`

"The message seems to include an employee's personal details. Remove them
and ask again in general terms."

blueprnt checks your message for what looks like an employee's full name
before it is ever sent to the model, and refuses it if it finds one; no AI
call happens for a refused message. Rephrase your question in general
terms, for example by role or group rather than by name. See
[Assistant privacy](/docs/assistant-privacy).
