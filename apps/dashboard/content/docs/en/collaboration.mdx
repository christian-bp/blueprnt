Collaboration (samverkan) is the first of the pay mapping's four analysis chapters, shown in the sidebar as "Collaboration", with "Get started and collaboration"
as its fuller name.

## Why the law requires it

Swedish law requires employers to carry out the active measures behind a
pay mapping together with employees, normally through their union
representatives, through every step: investigating, analyzing, acting, and
following up. Where a collective agreement applies, the union also has a
right to the information it needs to take part. This is a requirement in
its own right, not a formality: it is why a pay mapping cannot be completed
without a collaboration record on file.

## Recording collaboration

The chapter asks two plain questions: "Who takes part in the
collaboration?" and "How does the collaboration happen?", each a free-text
field. A third free-text field, "Remarks from the parties", records what
the parties said about the mapping; it is optional and prints in the
detail appendix, never in the signing report. A date picker under the
fields records the collaboration date; it is optional, saves the moment
you pick a day, and prints on the signing report. Everything you type
saves automatically as you go; there is no separate save action.
Continuing to the next chapter is never blocked on these fields, but a
hint reminds you that "the pay mapping cannot be
completed until both collaboration fields are filled in", so you can move
on and come back to them later.

## Completing the record

The pay mapping cannot be completed until both fields contain text; see
[Pay mapping lifecycle and statuses](/docs/run-lifecycle) for the rest of
that requirement.
