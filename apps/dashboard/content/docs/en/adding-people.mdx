Adding a person by hand is the fast path for a single new employee; bringing
in many at once is covered in [Importing people](/docs/importing-people).
Start from "Add employee" on the [People](/people) page.

## Identity fields

Name and gender are required; everything else can be added later. The
optional fields are employee number, department, start date, and FTE
percent. An employee number is not required, but once you set one it must
stay unique across your organization, because future payroll imports use it
to match this same person instead of creating a duplicate.

## Classifying while you add

The dialog also lets you pick a role and seniority for the person right
away, though neither is required at this point; you can classify them later
instead, from [Classifying people](/docs/classifying-people). A seniority
must be valid for the picked role's track, so picking a role on a different
track resets the seniority choice back to that track's first option.

## No salary here

This form never collects pay. Salary is entered afterward, on the person's
own page, once they exist; see
[Person details and salary](/docs/person-details-and-salary). Keeping salary
off this form keeps identity and pay as separate steps, and role data never
carries a person's pay either way.

## After adding

Saving takes you straight to the new person's page. If you picked a role and
seniority but that part of the save fails, the person is still created; you
can classify them afterward from Classify.
