blueprnt is built for European pay-transparency compliance, so it handles
EU employees' personal data. All of that data is kept within the EU.

## Where your data lives

Your organization's data, roles, evaluation model, people, salary
records, pay mappings, and the audit log, is stored in a managed database
operated by Convex inside the EU, in Ireland. Sign-in and organization
membership are held in that same EU database, so your identity data never
leaves the EU either.

The application itself, the pages you load in your browser, is served
from inside the EU as well, from Ireland. Notification emails, such as
invitations and password resets, go out through an email provider held to
the same EU-hosting requirement.

## AI stays in the EU too

Every AI feature in blueprnt, from drafting a job profile to the
assistant's replies, calls a model hosted in the EU. Your organization's
data never leaves the EU to generate an AI response. See
[How AI is used](/docs/how-ai-is-used) for what those features do and do
not send to the model.

## A regional choice, not a promise of full sovereignty

Keeping everything in the EU satisfies data-residency requirements, but
it runs on infrastructure operated by companies headquartered outside the
EU. If your organization needs a stricter, EU-sovereign-only setup, talk
to your account contact; this is not blueprnt's default configuration
today.
