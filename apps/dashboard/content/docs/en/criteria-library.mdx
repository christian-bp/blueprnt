blueprnt does not ship a ready-made model. It ships a library of 22
criteria, and building your model is choosing from it. Every criterion
belongs to one of the four dimensions the law names, and its definition,
its anchors and its control question are the library's, identical in every
organization, so two companies that chose the same criterion mean the same
thing by it.

This page is the whole library at a glance. The full definition of each
one, what it measures and does not measure, when it fits and when it does
not, opens in the picker on the
[Criteria chapter](/model/criteria) as you choose.

## Competence

- **Knowledge depth and specialist level.** Deep specialist knowledge within a bounded field.
- **Knowledge breadth and cross-disciplinary understanding.** The ability to connect several knowledge areas and understand how they relate.
- **Formal qualification, authorisation and certification requirements.** Mandatory licence, authorisation or certification.
- **Domain and business knowledge.** Deep knowledge of the industry, product, customer environment or business context.
- **Advisory and judgment competence.** Qualified advice and professional judgment as a basis for other people's decisions.

## Effort and complexity

- **Complexity and ambiguity.** Difficulty, uncertainty and ambiguity in the questions that have to be handled.
- **Analytical and problem-solving effort.** The extent of systematic analysis, troubleshooting and problem solving.
- **Communication and relationship-demanding work.** Requirements for qualified communication, negotiation and handling conflicting interests.
- **Operational intensity and simultaneous demands.** Requirements to handle several simultaneous flows and prioritise continuously.
- **Physical or sensory effort.** Recurring physical load, precision or sustained sensory concentration.
- **Cognitive endurance and focus load.** Requirements for sustained mental concentration and deep focus load.

## Responsibility and impact

- **Scope and impact.** The reach of results and impact in the business.
- **Autonomy and decision mandate.** Independence and the mandate to make trade-offs and take decisions.
- **Risk and consequence.** The seriousness of the possible consequences of errors, shortcomings or incorrect decisions.
- **Leadership and people responsibility.** Responsibility for leading people, coordinating activity and creating results through others.
- **Resource and capacity responsibility.** Responsibility for prioritising limited resources between the needs of the business.
- **Business and customer responsibility.** Responsibility for important customers, revenue or business results.
- **Information, security or compliance responsibility.** Formal responsibility for control, protection, quality assurance or regulatory compliance.

## Working conditions

- **Safety and exposure conditions.** Lasting exposure to physical, chemical, biological or environmental risks.
- **On-call, standby and availability requirements.** Recurring on-call duty, standby or requirements for rapid availability.
- **Irregularity, mobility and place-bound work.** Lasting requirements for irregular hours, travel or work at particular places.
- **Special security, confidentiality or controlled environments.** Work under special rules for access, confidentiality, security or control.

## Choosing from the library

A model holds 6 to 8 criteria, and no dimension may hold more than its
own cap: 2 for competence, 2 for effort and complexity, 3 for
responsibility and impact, and 1 for working conditions. The picker
refuses a criterion past a cap rather than warning about it afterwards.
Working conditions is the one dimension that may hold none, and only after
you have answered whether it materially applies.

## Where criteria overlap

Some criteria measure neighbouring things, and choosing both risks paying
for the same requirement twice. The library declares those pairs in
advance. Choosing both members of one does not block anything, but the
approval checklist asks you to record what you decided the boundary
between them is, and that note goes into the method appendix.

- Knowledge depth and specialist level and Knowledge breadth and cross-disciplinary understanding
- Knowledge depth and specialist level and Formal qualification, authorisation and certification requirements
- Knowledge depth and specialist level and Domain and business knowledge
- Knowledge depth and specialist level and Advisory and judgment competence
- Complexity and ambiguity and Analytical and problem-solving effort
- Analytical and problem-solving effort and Cognitive endurance and focus load
- Physical or sensory effort and Safety and exposure conditions
- Scope and impact and Leadership and people responsibility
- Risk and consequence and Information, security or compliance responsibility
- Information, security or compliance responsibility and Special security, confidentiality or controlled environments
- Scope and impact and Autonomy and decision mandate
- Autonomy and decision mandate and Leadership and people responsibility
- Autonomy and decision mandate and Resource and capacity responsibility
- Leadership and people responsibility and Resource and capacity responsibility
- Scope and impact and Business and customer responsibility
- On-call, standby and availability requirements and Irregularity, mobility and place-bound work
