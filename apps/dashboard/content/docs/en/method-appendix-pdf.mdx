The method appendix is a downloadable document that collects your
model's criteria, weights, and the rationale and bias review you
documented on the [Model](/model) page's Method tab. It is your model's compliance evidence for
the pay-transparency requirements, built entirely from model-level
content; it never contains any person's name, salary, or performance.

## What it contains

The document opens with a cover page and a methodology section
explaining how roles are evaluated: criterion by criterion on the 0 to 5
scale, weights summing to a fixed point budget, the weighting normalized
to 0 to 100 with the level derived rather than set by hand, ratings hidden
until every criterion is rated, and every change recorded in the audit
log. It states plainly that the model is designed to reduce bias and is
bias-reducing, never bias-free. After that comes the point budget with
every criterion's weight and share, then each criterion's rationale and
bias review, and finally the level thresholds.

## Draft or final

The document labels itself automatically: "DRAFT: not all criteria are
reviewed and approved" while any criterion is not yet approved, or
"FINAL" once every criterion on the Model page's Method tab is approved. Exporting is never blocked while criteria are still being
documented; the label is how the document is honest about how complete
its evidence is at the time you exported it.

## Exporting

"Download as PDF" on the Model page's Method tab produces the document on the spot,
built from what is currently saved; there is nothing to configure
beforehand beyond documenting your criteria as far as you want them
before exporting. Exporting only reads what you have already documented,
so it does not appear in the audit log. Export it whenever you need a
snapshot of your compliance
evidence, whether that is a work-in-progress DRAFT to share internally or
a FINAL version once every criterion is approved.
