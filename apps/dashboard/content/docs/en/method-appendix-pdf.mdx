The method appendix is a downloadable document that collects your
model's criteria, weights, and the rationale and bias review you
documented in the [Method chapter](/model/method) of the Model area. It
is your model's compliance evidence for
the pay-transparency requirements, built entirely from model-level
content; it never contains any person's name, salary, or performance.

## What it contains

The document opens with a cover page, a contents page, and a
methodology section explaining how roles are evaluated: criterion by criterion on the shared
1 to 5 scale, weights summing to a fixed point budget, the weighting
normalized to 0 to 100 with the level derived rather than set by hand,
the four zones and the profile requirement that can lower a placement
but never lift it, ratings hidden until every criterion is rated, and every change recorded
in the audit log. It states plainly that the model is designed to reduce
bias and is bias-reducing, never bias-free. After that come four more
sections. "The shared assessment scale" gives the five steps with their
meanings, and notes that steps 2 and 4 are considered midpoints between
the anchored steps around them. "Criteria and weights" is the point
budget with every criterion's weight and share. "Criterion rationale and
bias review" carries, per criterion, its definition, its full anchor
ladder, the rationale and bias review you wrote, and who approved it.
"Levels and zones" groups the twelve thresholds under their four zones
and writes out each zone's profile requirement in words, or says that
the weighting alone decides. Last comes "The working-conditions
materiality test", with the decision and its motivation.

## Draft or final

The document labels itself automatically: "DRAFT: not all criteria are
reviewed and approved" while any criterion is not yet approved, or
"FINAL" once every criterion in the Method chapter is approved. Exporting is never blocked while criteria are still being
documented; the label is how the document is honest about how complete
its evidence is at the time you exported it.

## Exporting

The appendix has its own card on the
[Approval chapter](/model/approval), above the approval gate, because the
document is the evidence of exactly what approving certifies. The card
names the document, shows whether it is DRAFT or FINAL, and carries
"Download as PDF", which produces the document on the spot from what is
currently saved; there is nothing to configure
beforehand beyond documenting your criteria as far as you want them
before exporting. Exporting only reads what you have already documented,
so it does not appear in the audit log. Export it whenever you need a
snapshot of your compliance
evidence, whether that is a work-in-progress DRAFT to share internally or
a FINAL version once every criterion is approved.
