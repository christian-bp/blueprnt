Admins add new members to the organization by inviting them, from the
Members tab of the Organization page.

## Inviting someone

The "Invite member" button opens a dialog to send an email invitation to
join the organization. Enter the person's email address, choose their role,
Admin or Editor, and confirm with "Send invitation".

## While the invitation is pending

Once sent, the invitation appears in the members list marked "Pending"
until the person accepts it. It is not yet a membership: the invited person
has no access to the organization until they act on it.

## Revoking an invitation

A pending invitation's "..." menu offers "Revoke". Confirming asks "Revoke
this invitation?" and, once confirmed, the invitation link stops working
immediately. You can invite the same person again later if needed.

## Accepting an invitation

The invited person receives an email with a link to accept the invitation.
Opening it while already signed in to their blueprnt account shows an
"Accept invitation" step; accepting adds them to the organization with the role you
assigned and takes them to the Overview page. Opening it while signed out
shows a prompt to sign in first, then they return to the same link to
accept. blueprnt has no public sign-up: the invited person needs an
existing blueprnt account to sign in with, so someone without one yet still
needs an account provisioned for them before they can accept.
