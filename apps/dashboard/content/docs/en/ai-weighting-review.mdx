From the top row of the [Model](/model) area's [Weighting
chapter](/model/weighting), AI can review your current weighting and
propose adjustments for you to accept or decline. It never changes your
model on its own.

## When the review is available

The "Review" button sits on the chapter's top row, beside "Save
weighting" when there is something to save. It is only offered while
your saved weighting is what the AI would see: with unsaved weight
changes it goes away until you save or undo them. If you have just
applied a review and have not changed the weighting since, it also
stays away until you make a new change, so a review is never requested
against a weighting it has already reviewed.

## What the AI proposes

Choosing "Review" opens a panel that requests a set of balanced moves
straight away, each one written as
moving a number of weight points from one criterion to another, together
with a short motivation for why. Every individual move is itself
zero-sum (points removed from one criterion always land on another), so
the overall allocation stays exactly on budget no matter which moves you
end up choosing. If the AI finds nothing worth adjusting, it says so
("The AI found no moves to suggest.") and offers only a way to dismiss.

## What the AI is shown, and what it is never shown

The review sees your organization's profile (industry, country, size),
your criteria with their current weight points, any weight motivations
you have written, your working-conditions decision, and your roles
grouped into role families with a sample of titles from each. That is
what lets it argue about your company rather than about companies in
general.

It is never shown a single person, and it is never shown a result. No
salaries, no names, no employment data, and no role's weighting, level,
or zone. That is deliberate and enforced in code, not only in the
instructions the AI is given: an AI that could see where your roles land
could tune the method toward those placements, and the method has to be
argued from the work instead.

## Choosing which moves to accept

Each proposed move has its own checkbox and starts checked; you can
uncheck any move you disagree with before applying. A "Why this
change?" disclosure on each move reveals its motivation without
cluttering the list. "Apply selected" applies only the moves you left
checked; "Dismiss" discards the whole review without changing anything.

## Provenance and confirmation

Every review is labelled as an AI-generated suggestion that you review
and confirm; nothing from it is ever applied automatically. The audit
log records an applied review as an AI suggestion you confirmed, not as
an ordinary manual edit, so the model's history always shows which
changes came from an AI review and which weight points you accepted.
