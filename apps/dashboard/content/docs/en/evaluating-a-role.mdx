title: Evaluating a role

Evaluating a role means rating it, one criterion at a time, against
every criterion in your [evaluation model](/model). blueprnt then
derives the role's weighting and level from those ratings once you complete
them; neither is ever typed in directly or overridden.

## Before you can rate

Two things need to be true first. Your organization's model needs to be
approved: until then, rating states that the model needs approving
first, with a link to the Approval chapter; see [Method
documentation](/docs/method-documentation). A role also needs a complete
[job profile](/docs/job-profiles), its purpose and responsibilities
filled in; open the role from [Roles](/roles), and until its profile is
complete, its evaluation area shows "Complete the job profile first"
instead of a way to rate it.

## Rating one criterion at a time

From the role, choose "Rate role" (or "Continue rating" if you already
started). Rating shows one criterion at a time, in the model's order:
its name, an assessment question, its measures and what it does not
measure as expandable context, and its fixed anchor texts at steps 1, 3,
and 5 (with a considered midpoint at 2 and 4) as selectable options,
plus an always-present motivation field. A working-conditions criterion
also offers "The role is not covered by this working condition" as an
option alongside the five steps, for a role the condition simply does
not apply to. A progress line at the top reads "Criterion X of Y" so you
always know how far through you are. Above the options, "Shared assessment
scale" names the step each option belongs to and opens what all five steps
mean; see [Criteria and the evaluation scale](/docs/criteria-and-scale).

A motivation is required whenever you choose step 1, 4, or 5; steps 2
and 3, and the not-covered option, do not need one. Pressing a digit key
selects the matching step, and Enter saves it and moves on, if you
prefer the keyboard to clicking. "Next criterion" saves the step you
picked before advancing; on the last criterion the same button reads
"Finish rating" instead. "Back" moves to the previous criterion without
saving anything.

To keep ratings honest, the weighting, the level, and every other
criterion's rating stay hidden throughout: each criterion is judged on
its own merits, never to hit a total. Opening a role you have already
started rating always begins at the first criterion, with every answer
you have already given still selected, so walking forward over answered
ground costs a press rather than a re-read.

## Completing is the reveal

Nothing is shown while you rate. The last step of the flow carries
"Complete the assessment", and that one press saves the last rating and
reveals the role's weighting, level, and, if your organization has active
anchor roles, how it compares to them. The step says so before you press
it. Completing is a deliberate act rather than something that happens on
its own, so the result only ever appears once you choose to see it. See
[Weighting and levels](/docs/score-and-levels) for how these numbers are
computed and what they mean.

## Changing a rating later

A completed assessment has to be reopened before any of its ratings can
change. "Re-evaluate" does that in one press, from the result or from the
role's actions menu; it keeps every rating and hides the weighting and
level again until you complete the assessment once more. A completed role
shows "Assessed under a previous method" whenever it was completed before
your organization's current method approval, or whenever that approval is
currently reopened at all, the common case right after any
method-affecting edit; it is a prompt to evaluate the role again under the
current method rather than a blocker. See
[Adjusting a rating](/docs/adjusting-a-rating) for how reopening and
re-rating work, and when a role is blocked from either.
