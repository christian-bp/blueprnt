title: Evaluating a role
description: How rating a role against its criteria produces its weighting and level.

Evaluating a role means rating it, one criterion at a time, against every
criterion in your [evaluation model](/model). blueprnt then derives the
role's weighting and level from those ratings; neither is ever typed in
directly or overridden.

## Before you can rate

A role needs a complete [job profile](/docs/job-profiles), its purpose and
responsibilities filled in, before rating can start. Open the role from
[Roles](/roles); until the profile is complete, its evaluation area shows
"Complete the job profile first" instead of a way to rate it, telling you
to fill in purpose and responsibilities before rating.

## Rating one criterion at a time

From the role, choose "Rate role" (or "Continue rating" if you already
started). Rating shows one criterion at a time, in the model's order: its
name, a description, any extra help text, and its six 0-5 steps as
selectable options, plus an always-present optional motivation field for
why you chose that step. A progress line at the top reads "Criterion X of
Y" so you always know how far through you are.

Pressing a digit key 0-5 selects the matching step, and Enter saves it and
moves on, if you prefer the keyboard to clicking. "Next criterion" saves
the step you picked before advancing; on the last criterion the same
button reads "Show result" instead. "Back" moves to the previous criterion
without saving anything.

To keep ratings honest, the weighting, the level, and every other
criterion's rating stay hidden throughout: each criterion is judged on its
own merits, never to hit a total. Reopening a role you started rating
resumes at the first criterion you have not yet rated.

## Seeing the result

Once every criterion has a rating, the result appears: the role's
weighting out of 100 and its level. If your organization has active
anchor roles, they are listed alongside the result so you can sanity
check the new role's level against roles you already trust. See
[Weighting and levels](/docs/score-and-levels) for how these two numbers are
computed and what they mean.

## Changing a rating later

A role stays open for rating at any time until it is archived. Choose
"Adjust ratings" from the role's menu to revisit any criterion; see
[Adjusting a rating](/docs/adjusting-a-rating) for how that works and when
it is blocked.
