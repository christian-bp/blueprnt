The people register lists every employee your organization has imported or
added, whether or not they are classified into a role yet. Open it from
[People](/people); the register itself is the "Directory" tab, alongside a
"Classify" tab covered in [Classifying people](/docs/classifying-people).

## Reading the table

Each row shows the person's name, gender, department, FTE, role, and
seniority. The table sorts by name by default and shows 25 people per page,
with paging controls once there are more. A person whose role or seniority
is still an unconfirmed suggestion shows a "Suggested" badge next to it; the
badge's tooltip explains that the role is not confirmed yet and links the
idea to the Classify tab.

## Searching and filtering

The search field matches by name or department. Separate filters narrow by
department, role, gender, and FTE (all employees, full-time only, or
part-time only); the role filter lists every role you have created, not only
roles that already have people. A person with no role assigned matches no
specific role filter, only "All roles". Once you search or filter, a result
count such as "12 of 40 people" appears, along with a way to clear the
filters.

## Selecting rows and bulk delete

Checking rows selects them; the header checkbox only selects the people
currently visible on the page, but your selection is kept as you page or
filter, so a selection can span more people than are on screen at once.
Once anything is selected, a delete action appears, naming how many
employees are selected; it permanently deletes everyone selected, across
every page, not just the visible ones. See [Erasing a person](/docs/erasing-a-person) for what this
does and how it is confirmed.

## Adding and importing

Two actions sit above the table. "Add employee" opens a dialog for a single
person; see [Adding people](/docs/adding-people). "Import salaries" opens
the import wizard for bringing in a payroll export at once; see
[Importing people](/docs/importing-people).
