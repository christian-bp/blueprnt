The people register lists every employee your organization has imported or
added, whether or not they are classified into a role yet. Open it from
[People](/people); the register itself is the Directory page, alongside a
Classify page covered in [Classifying people](/docs/classifying-people).

## Reading the table

Each row shows the person's name, gender, department, FTE, role, and
seniority. The table sorts by name by default and shows 25 people per page,
with paging controls once there are more and a page-size choice of 25, 50,
or 100. A person whose role or seniority
is still an unconfirmed suggestion shows a "Suggested" badge next to it;
hovering the badge explains that the role is not confirmed yet and points
you to the Classify page to confirm it. A person who has left shows an
"Archived" badge next to their name and appears only when the status
control shows archived employees.

## Searching and filtering

The search field matches by name or department. Separate filters narrow by
department, role, gender, and FTE (all employees, full-time only, or
part-time only), and a status control shows either active employees (the
default) or archived employees; the role filter lists every role you have
created, not only roles that already have people. A person with no role
assigned matches no specific role filter, only "All roles". Once you search
or filter, a result count such as "12 of 40 people" appears, along with a
way to clear the filters.

## Selecting rows

Checking rows selects them; the header checkbox only selects the people
currently visible on the page, but your selection is kept as you page or
filter, so a selection can span more people than are on screen at once.
What the selection can do depends on the view. Among active employees the
only action is to archive them. Among archived employees you can reactivate
them, or, as an admin, delete them permanently; see
[Erasing a person](/docs/erasing-a-person) for what that does and how it is
confirmed.

## Archiving employees who have left

Someone who has left the organization is archived, not deleted: select their
rows and choose the archive action naming how many employees are selected.
The dialog states what archiving does and archives them in batches with a
live count. An archived person leaves the active register, classification,
pay mappings and counts, keeps their salary history and role assignment, and
can be reactivated from their own page. Archiving is open to every member.
Deleting is possible only for an archived employee, from the archived view
or from the person's own page, and only for an admin. A payroll import can
also archive people who are missing from the file; see
[Importing people](/docs/importing-people).

## Adding and importing

Two actions sit above the table. "Add employee" opens a dialog for a single
person; see [Adding people](/docs/adding-people). "Import" opens the Import
people wizard for bringing in a payroll export at once; see
[Importing people](/docs/importing-people).
