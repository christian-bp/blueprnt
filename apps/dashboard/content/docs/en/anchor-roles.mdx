An anchor role is a reference role your organization has assessed and agreed
on, used to sanity check other roles' evaluations. It is decision support: a
comparison that helps you judge whether a new result looks right, never a
replacement for rating a role against the criteria.

Do not confuse an anchor role with the anchor texts on a criterion's 0 to 5
scale (also called an "anchor" in the model). An anchor role is a whole role
your organization has designated as a reference; the criterion's anchors are
the wording that describes each of its six steps. See
[Criteria and the evaluation scale](/docs/criteria-and-scale) for the latter.

## Designating one

Designating and managing anchor roles is admin-only. From a fully evaluated
role's actions menu, choose "Designate as anchor role", set the agreed level,
and write a motivation for why this role is a good reference point. A role
must have a complete evaluation before it can be designated, since an anchor
needs its own computed level to be useful as a reference.

blueprnt suggests keeping 2 to 5 anchor roles in total; this is guidance
shown in the dialog, not an enforced limit.

## Status and the deviation flag

An anchor role's status is "Active", "Under review", or "Replaced"; an admin
can change it from "Manage anchor role" on the role's actions menu. A
replaced anchor is never deleted, so its history stays visible.

Once designated, the role's evaluation area shows the agreed level and
motivation alongside the level blueprnt computed from the role's
evaluation. If the two differ, a deviation badge appears, flagging that
the computed level no longer matches the agreed one; the computed level
always remains the one that counts, and the deviation is a prompt to look
again, not an override.
