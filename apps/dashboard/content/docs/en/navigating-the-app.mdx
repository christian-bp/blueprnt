The sidebar on the left is grouped into four sections: Status, Job
evaluation, People & pay, and, for admins only, Administration. This page
walks through every destination in order.

## Status

**[Overview](/)** is the dashboard landing page. It greets you, shows a
short status line of what is left to do, and gives you a row of action
cards: outstanding work first, then standing shortcuts such as importing
employees or starting a pay mapping, so the row is never empty.

**[Assistant](/assistant)** opens the blueprnt AI chat. It can help with
concepts, your organization's own numbers, and where to do things in the
app, but it never accepts personal data such as names or salaries in a
question.

**[Documentation](/docs)** is this section: the guide you are reading now.

## Job evaluation

**Job architecture** covers how your roles compare to each other. Its
**[Levels](/work)** view lists roles by their computed level, with a
matrix view that crosses level against track, and a families view that
groups the same data by role family. Its **[Roles](/roles)** view is the
role register: every role's job profile, track, and evaluation status.

**[Model](/model)** is where your evaluation model lives, as a guided
journey of four chapters under one progress bar:
**[Criteria](/model/criteria)** (choose what every role is judged
against, from blueprnt's library), **[Weighting](/model/weighting)** (set
how much each one counts), **[Method](/model/method)** (document why each
belongs), and **[Approval](/model/approval)** (check and approve the
model). Opening the area lands you on the first chapter.

## People & pay

**[People](/people)** is the employee register, across two tabs: Directory
(every imported person, their pay, and their current role) and
**[Classify](/people/classify)** (assign or confirm which role and
seniority each person holds).

**[Pay mappings](/pay-mappings)** lists every pay mapping you have started,
and is where you start a new one. Once a mapping is open it has its own
tabs: **Overview** (the frozen data the mapping was started from),
**Analysis** (the statutory analysis, which opens with the collaboration
step), **Actions** (the action plan), and **Report**, where the signable
summary and exports will appear.

## Administration

These destinations only appear in the sidebar for organization admins.

**[Organization](/organization)** covers your organization's own settings,
across two tabs: **[General](/organization/general)** (name, country,
currency, language, industry, logo) and
**[Members](/organization/members)** (who has access, their role, and
pending invitations).

**[Audit log](/audit-log)** lists every change in your organization, who
made it, and when.
