The app's frame has three parts: a narrow icon rail along the left edge,
a section sidebar beside it on areas that hold several pages, and a top
bar. Every page opens with a breadcrumb trail naming where you are, and
each crumb links back up a level.

## The icon rail

The rail lists the app's areas as icons; hover one to see its name. From
the top: **[Home](/)**,
**[Job architecture](/work)**, **[Model](/model)**, **[People](/people)**,
and **[Pay mappings](/pay-mappings)**. At the bottom sit
**[Documentation](/docs)**, **Settings**, and your avatar, which opens
the account menu. Areas an admin manages (the organization pages, the
audit log) appear inside Settings only for admins.

## The section sidebar

Every area opens with a second navigation column next to the rail, its
pages grouped under small headings; Home's column is also where the
**[Assistant](/assistant)** lives. The handle on the column's right edge
collapses it when you want the room and brings it back the same way.
Three surfaces fill this column with their own content instead: the
Assistant shows your conversation list, the Documentation shows the
guide's chapters, and an open pay mapping shows that mapping's own pages
with a switcher between your mappings.

At the foot of the column, a small "To do" block glances your
outstanding work: the top items as rows linking straight to where each
one is done, and a finished item celebrates before its row slides away.
Two areas stack a block of their own above it: People splits the
workforce across Confirmed, Pending, and Unclassified, and an open pay
mapping shows its own frozen headcount and pay gap as "Key figures". A
row in the column can also carry a live count, such as how many roles
are left to evaluate or how many people are left to classify.

## The top bar

The organization switcher sits on the left; if you belong to several
organizations, this is where you change between them. The search field
in the middle opens the command palette (also Cmd+K or Ctrl+K), which
jumps to any page and searches this guide, down to its individual
sections. The Add button on
the right starts the most common additions from anywhere: a new role, a
role or people import, an invitation.

## Home, Assistant, and Documentation

**[Home](/)** is the landing page. It greets you, shows a short
status line of what is left to do, and gives you a row of action cards:
outstanding work first, then standing shortcuts such as importing
employees or starting a pay mapping, so the row is never empty. Below
the cards, three figures (workforce, roles, and the latest pay gap)
each link to their own area.

**[Assistant](/assistant)**, reached from Home's sidebar, opens the
blueprnt AI chat. It can help with
concepts, your organization's own numbers, and where to do things in the
app, but it never accepts personal data such as names or salaries in a
question.

**[Documentation](/docs)** is this section: the guide you are reading now.

## Job architecture

**Job architecture** covers how your roles compare to each other. Its
**[Levels](/work)** page lists roles by their computed level, with a
matrix view that crosses level against track, and a families view that
groups the same data by role family. Its **[Roles](/roles)** page is the
role register: every role's job profile, track, and evaluation status.

## Model

**[Model](/model)** is where your evaluation model lives, as a guided
journey of four chapters, tracked by one four-segment instrument on its
title row:
**[Criteria](/model/criteria)** (choose what every role is judged
against, from blueprnt's library), **[Weighting](/model/weighting)** (set
how much each one counts), **[Method](/model/method)** (document why each
belongs), and **[Approval](/model/approval)** (check and approve the
model). The four are the area's own sidebar rows, and opening the
area lands you on the first chapter.

## People and pay

**[People](/people)** is the employee area. Its **Directory** page is
the register of every imported person, their pay, and their current
role; its **[Classify](/people/classify)** page is where you assign or
confirm which role and seniority each person holds.

**[Pay mappings](/pay-mappings)** lists every pay mapping you have
started, and is where you start a new one. Once a mapping is open, its
sidebar carries the mapping's own pages: **Overview** (the frozen data
the mapping was started from), **Analysis** (the statutory analysis,
which opens with the collaboration step), **Actions** (the action plan),
and **Report**, where the signable summary and exports will appear.

## Settings

The gear at the bottom of the rail collects everything about the account
and the organization. Admins see the **Organization** group:
**[General](/organization/general)** (name, country, currency, language,
industry, logo) and **[Members](/organization/members)** (who has
access, their role, and pending invitations). Everyone sees **Account
settings**: **[Profile](/account/profile)** and
**[Security](/account/security)**. **[Audit log](/audit-log)**, admins
only, lists every change in your organization, who made it, and when.
