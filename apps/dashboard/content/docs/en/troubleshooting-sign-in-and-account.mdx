title: Permission, membership, and account errors
description: What each permission, membership, and account message means, and what to do about it.
section: troubleshooting
---

blueprnt never shows raw error text from the server; every message you see
here is one of a fixed set of codes translated into plain language. This
page explains each one that can appear while working across organizations
or managing your own account. It does not cover the sign-in form itself
(a wrong password, an invalid two-factor code, or an expired link), since
those are plain messages from the sign-in screen, not codes from this
system.

## I don't have permission to do this

### adminRequired

Code: `adminRequired`

"Only organization admins can do this."

Some actions, such as changing the evaluation model or organization
settings, or managing members, are restricted to the Admin role. This
appears if you attempt one of them as an Editor. Ask an organization admin
to make the change, or to promote you to Admin first. See
[Members and roles](/docs/members-and-roles) for the exact boundary between
the two roles.

### platformAdminRequired

Code: `platformAdminRequired`

"You do not have platform admin access."

A small set of actions are restricted to blueprnt's own platform
administrators, a separate flag from any organization's Admin role. This is
not something an organization admin can grant themselves or another
member; it is not self-serviceable.

## I'm told I'm not a member of this organization

### notAMember

Code: `notAMember`

"You are not a member of this organization."

This appears if you try to view or act on data for an organization you do
not currently belong to, for example after following an old link or
switching accounts. Switch to an organization you belong to, or ask an
admin of that organization to invite you; see [Invitations](/docs/invitations).

### membershipConflict

Code: `membershipConflict`

"Your membership could not be verified. Contact support."

This is a rare state where blueprnt cannot resolve which organization your
membership belongs to, or finds more than one conflicting record. Unlike
the other messages on this page, there is no self-service fix: contact
support.

## I can't delete my account, or I can't remove or demote the last administrator

### lastAdmin

Code: `lastAdmin`

"You're the last administrator of an organization. Contact support to
delete your account."

An organization can never be left without an admin, since there would be
no way back in to fix it from inside the app. This blocks three related
actions: deleting your own account while you are the sole admin of an
organization, demoting the last admin to Editor, and removing the last
admin from the organization. Promote another member to Admin first (see
[Members and roles](/docs/members-and-roles)), after which the original
admin can be changed, removed, or their account deleted like anyone
else's. See [Deleting your account](/docs/deleting-your-account) for the
rest of what account deletion does.

## General

These codes are not specific to any one screen; they can appear anywhere
in the app.

### notAuthenticated

Code: `notAuthenticated`

"You need to sign in."

Your session is no longer valid, most often because it expired or you
signed out in another tab. Sign in again to continue.

### notFound

Code: `notFound`

"Not found."

The item you were viewing or acting on no longer exists from your
organization's point of view, most often because it was deleted or
archived (by you or a colleague) since the page loaded. Reload the page;
if the item is genuinely gone, it will no longer appear.

### invalidInput

Code: `invalidInput`

"Invalid input."

A generic message for a value that failed validation before it reached
any more specific check, such as a name that is too long, or exceeding a
bulk-import cap. Check the field you last changed against any hint text
shown near it, and correct it.
