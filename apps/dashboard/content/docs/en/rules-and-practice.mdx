Rules and practice (bestämmelser och praxis) is the pay mapping's second
analysis chapter, shown in the sidebar as "Practice". Before comparing anyone's pay, the law
requires reviewing the rules and practice you actually apply to pay, on
gender-neutral grounds.

## The areas reviewed

Four areas apply to every pay mapping, each its own step with a plain
question: pay policy ("Are the pay policy and pay-setting criteria
gender-neutral, and are they clear to those who apply them?"), collective
agreements ("Are collective agreements and local pay agreements applied
the same way for women and men?"), benefits and variable pay ("Are
benefits, allowances and variable pay granted on gender-neutral grounds?"),
and pay-setting practice ("Is pay-setting practice gender-neutral at
hiring, promotion and during parental leave?").

A fifth area, previous actions, appears only once your organization has an
earlier, completed pay mapping with an earlier reference date: "How were
the measures planned in the previous pay mapping carried out?" It asks
whether last year's planned measures were actually carried out and whether
they had the intended effect, the annual follow-up the law expects.

## Recording a verdict

Each area is answered with one of two choices, "No deficiencies found" or
"Deficiencies or unclarities found", and then closed with "Mark done and
continue". Choosing the second requires a description first; choosing the
first does not. Changing an already-recorded verdict, or clearing a
required description, reopens the area, and "Undo mark as done" reopens it
deliberately, so a mark of "done" always matches what is currently on
file.
