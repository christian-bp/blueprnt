Equivalent work (likvärdigt arbete) is the pay mapping's fourth analysis
chapter, tabbed as "Women-dominated groups" and shown as "Equivalent work"
in the shorter progress bar. The law requires comparing a
women-dominated group against work that is valued as equivalent or lower
but still pays more.

## Women-dominated groups

Here, a group is the same grouping equal work compares within: everyone
sharing the same role title and level. It counts as women-dominated once
at least 60% of it is women, a guideline from the Swedish Equality
Ombudsman (DO) rather than a fixed number written into law. Because the
grouping is role title plus level, even a single woman in such a group
counts, being 100% women on her own.

## The comparator logic

For each women-dominated group, blueprnt compares its pay against every
other job the evaluation values as equal or lower, yet that still pays
more, the direction the law actually asks about. The comparator table
lists every such job grouped by level, starting at the women-dominated
group's own level and moving toward lower-valued ones, and within each
level ordered by the largest pay difference first, so the comparison that
most needs an explanation always leads its level. Each row shows its
level, headcount, share of women, average pay, and the difference against
the women-dominated group in both percent and currency. A group with
nothing to compare against shows "No equally or lower valued group
out-earns this one" and needs no documentation here.

## The scatter view

Below the table, a chart plots every person in the comparison by age or by
tenure, whichever you choose, against their FTE-adjusted pay, so you can
look for an objective pattern behind a difference rather than reading only
the averages. Selecting a row in the comparator table highlights just that
job's people in the chart.

## Documenting a comparison

You can attach an objective reason or a note to a women-dominated group as
a whole, or to one specific comparator row, the one higher-paid job it is
measured against, from that row's own menu; see
[Actions and notes](/docs/actions-and-notes) for what each of those
records. Once a group is documented, choose "Mark done and continue" to
close it and move to the next; "Undo mark as done" reopens it.
