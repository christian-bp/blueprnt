Wherever a finding shows up in the analysis, an action or a note lets you
record what you decided about it, without leaving the chapter you are in.

## Actions versus notes

An action carries a description of the problem, the planned action, an
optional objective reason, a responsible person chosen from your
organization's members, a planned date, an optional estimated cost, a
priority (High, Medium, or Low), and a status (Not started, In progress,
or Done).

A note is free text with one of three types: "Objective reason
documented", "Further discussion needed", or "No action needed". Use a
note when nothing needs to be acted on, and an action when something does.

Each group, person, or comparator row carries at most one action and one
note at a time; opening the menu on a target that already has one lets you
edit or delete it rather than create another.

## What a record can target

An action or note can target a group (in either the equal-work or
equivalent-work chapter), one specific comparator within a women-dominated
comparison, or a single person. An action or note attached to a person
shows that person's details as this pay mapping recorded them. It keeps
displaying correctly even if the person is later erased from your live
People register, and erasing them does not leave a copy of their details
behind in the action.

## The one overview table

The Actions tab gathers every action and note across the whole pay
mapping in one place, so following up later never requires walking back
through all four analysis chapters. Actions and notes list separately,
each with its own filters (status, priority, owner, comparison type, and
due date) and a search field, and a summary strip totals the actions and
their estimated cost. Every row's "Linked to" column names the group or
comparison it belongs to and opens straight back into that chapter. A
small badge also appears beside the group, person, or comparator row
itself wherever it is shown in the analysis, so you can see it already has
an action or a note before you ever open the Actions tab.

## Locking

Editing the content of an action or a note locks once the pay mapping is
completed, the same as the rest of the analysis. Changing an action's
status stays open even after completion, since the action plan itself is
meant to be followed up over the years it covers, up to the three years
the law allows for carrying out pay adjustments.
