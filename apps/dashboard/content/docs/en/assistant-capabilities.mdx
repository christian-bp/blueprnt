blueprnt AI answers three kinds of questions: what something means, what
your organization's own numbers currently are, and where to find something
in the app or this documentation.

## Explaining concepts

Ask it to explain a term such as criterion, level, seniority, or weighting,
and it answers in plain language using the same definitions this
documentation and the app itself use, including the boundaries between
terms that are easy to mix up, such as level (the outcome computed from a
role's weighting, compared against the method's level thresholds, with
level 1 the highest) versus seniority (an individual's own seniority, which
never changes a role's evaluation).

## Your organization's own numbers

Ask about your organization's current state and blueprnt AI can state
workforce size, how many roles exist, how many of them are evaluated, and
your latest pay gap percentage. It can also state average or median pay,
organization-wide or split by gender.

Every number it states is read live from your organization's own data at
the moment you ask, never guessed or drawn from general knowledge. Workforce
size and the pay gap figure come from your latest completed pay mapping's
frozen data, the same figures that mapping itself shows; role counts and pay
statistics are read from your live registers as they stand today. Because
these two sources can move independently between pay mappings, the
assistant is careful to state which basis a figure comes from rather than
mixing them.

## Charts

Ask how headcount or the pay gap has developed and blueprnt AI can draw a
trend chart of it, with one point per completed pay
mapping. The chart always shows today's numbers, so reopening an old
conversation shows your organization's current figures, not the ones from
when you first asked.

## Searching this documentation

blueprnt AI can also search this documentation and link you straight to the
page that answers a "how do I" question, such as how to run a pay mapping
or how weighting works, instead of only describing the steps itself.
