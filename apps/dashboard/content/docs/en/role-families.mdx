title: Role families
description: How role families group your register, and what happens when you rename or delete one.

A role family groups roles that share the same kind of work, such as
"Engineering" or "Sales". A family is purely organizational: it groups the
register and never affects a role's evaluation, weighting, or level. A role
belongs to at most one family, or none.

Do not confuse a family with a track. A track (individual contributor, lead,
manager) is the kind of job a role is; a family is how you choose to group
related roles for your own register. Two roles in the same family can sit on
different tracks.

## The family page

Selecting a family's name from the role register opens its page: the
family's roles in one table, sorted by track and then alphabetically by
title, with each role's evaluation progress and result shown under the same
visibility rules as the register (a value only appears once a role's
evaluation is complete).
The register's search and track filter work the same way here, scoped to the
family.

## Renaming and deleting

Both actions live in the family's actions menu on its page. Renaming changes
only the family's name; every family name must be unique in your
organization, regardless of case.

Deleting a family unfiles its roles. It never deletes or archives them: every
role that belonged to the family keeps all its data and moves to "No family"
in the register. The delete confirmation lists the roles that will be
unfiled, so the consequence is explicit before you confirm.
